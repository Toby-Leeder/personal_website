Hi, I'm Toby. I study **Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at UC Berkeley**, and I like building software end to end: schema, backend, UI, and the deploy pipeline that gets it in front of people.

Right now I'm a **technical product intern at a stealth AI startup** founded by Eric Schmidt and Sebastian Thrun, shipping features across Swift and Kotlin codebases for consumer social apps, building internal moderation and analytics dashboards on Supabase and Postgres, and wiring up a Raspberry Pi signup tracker to production APIs. I'm also a **CS 61A tutor at UC Berkeley**, supporting 30+ students in weekly lab sections. Before that I tutored Data C8 and spent a summer as an AI product intern at AskTuring AI.

## Outside of engineering

I grew up in San Diego with a twin brother and a younger brother. I've been to 15 countries so far, and Thailand is the all-time favorite.

I direct short films with the Business and Film Association at Berkeley, most recently leading a crew of 20 and a cast of 4 through production. Before that I spent two years with BareStage Productions as financial director, stage manager, sound and light designer, and actor, and performed in *The Prom* in summer 2025. I also tutor math, physics, and SAT prep.

I'm a bit of a gamer. On League of Legends I'm always up for a match, and in Teamfight Tactics I've climbed into the top 5% of players in North America.

## The fun fact

I was a child actor and model, and you might still spot me on an old billboard somewhere. I once shot an ad campaign in Buffy the Vampire Slayer's house, and you can catch me as a baby in season 3 of *The Office*.
